Introduction

Most incident time is spent searching, not fixing. LogSense compresses the search phase through intelligent grouping and prioritization.

Key Insights

  • Automatic clustering cuts repetitive scanning.
  • Summary views reduce handoff friction across team members.
  • Timeline-first analysis shortens root-cause discovery loops.

Examples and Tips

  • Use LogSense in every post-incident review to identify preventable delays.
  • Save reusable filters by service and error family.
  • Track MTTR before and after adoption to quantify gains.

Conclusion

Time savings compound quickly. Even small triage improvements become major engineering capacity wins.
