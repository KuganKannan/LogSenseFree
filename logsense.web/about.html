About LogSense

LogSense is a developer tool designed to help engineers understand massive log files quickly and identify the root cause of system issues.

The Problem

Too Many Logs

Modern systems generate huge amounts of logs every second. Finding useful information inside them can be extremely difficult.

Repeated Errors

The same stack trace may appear thousands of times, making debugging slow and frustrating.

Hidden Root Causes

The first failure is often buried inside millions of lines of logs.

The Idea Behind LogSense

LogSense was built to help developers quickly extract insights from large log datasets. Instead of manually reading through thousands of lines, LogSense analyzes patterns, clusters errors, and highlights the most important issues. The goal is simple: turn overwhelming logs into clear insights.
